Instead of showing every tool at once, use one workspace at a time. That keeps the dashboard calm while still giving you the controls to manage recurring items, real activity, accounts, goals, and settings.

Cash item

Add income, bills, or a one-time expense

Income: choose Income, enter the amount, and use the first pay date.

Expenses: choose Expense, enter the amount, and use the due date.

Schedule: use One time for a single payment, or choose a repeating schedule for recurring bills or paychecks.
